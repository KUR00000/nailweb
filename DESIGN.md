# Design System: Ivory Nails Atelier (Haute Couture Luxury)

## Overview

Ivory Nails Atelier represents Haute Couture Editorial Beauty. It eliminates generic AI-generated templates and stock clutter, replacing them with bespoke editorial asymmetry, high-fashion typography, authentic salon nail artistry, and tactile micro-interactions.

## Colors

- **Porcelain Ground (`#FBF9F7`)**: Warm gallery canvas tailored for true-to-life lacquer tones.
- **Charcoal Noir (`#1C1917`)**: High-contrast typography and primary couture buttons (AAA contrast ratio).
- **Muted Petal Blush & Dusty Rose (`#EBD7D3`, `#B57770`, `#94524B`)**: Restrained editorial accents for category badges, finish tags, and pricing highlights.
- **Atelier Gold (`#B8966E`)**: Flagship spotlight seals, star ratings, and VIP status indicators.
- **Emerald Green (`#2E6B49`)**: Reserved exclusively for in-stock and payment confirmation states.

## Typography

- **Display & Headings**: `Playfair Display`. Evokes vintage French cosmetic packaging and high-fashion editorial spreads.
- **Body & Controls**: Clean geometric sans-serif tuned for effortless legibility across all screen densities.
- **Editorial Micro-Labels**: Small caps with `letter-spacing: 0.12em` for luxury catalog classification.

## Visual Signatures

1. **Atelier Marquee Ribbon**: Continuous high-contrast announcement strip running across the top.
2. **Asymmetric Haute Couture Hero**: Contrast between an oversized manifesto on the left and an interactive Flagship Spotlight card on the right.
3. **Interactive Shade Palette Bar**: Direct color swatch picker allowing customers to filter the collection by aesthetic shade tone.
4. **Couture Product Cards**: Every item features authentic salon manicure photography, an individual color swatch dot, silhouette/volume tags, finish descriptions, and 1-tap bag addition.
5. **Salon Sizing & Wear Protocol Guide**: Illustrated 3-step guide detailing nail bed sizing, application options (tabs vs resin glue), and gentle removal.
6. **Connoisseur Review**: Editorial italic pull quote from Vogue Beauty curators.
